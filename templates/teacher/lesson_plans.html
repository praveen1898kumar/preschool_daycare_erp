Developmental Domains & Milestones

Track and award digital achievement badges to children

Cognitive & Language Domain 1
  • Recognizes and names 5 common animal sounds
  • Sorts items by primary color (Red, Blue, Yellow)
  • Counts independently up to 20 without repetition
Motor Skills (Fine & Gross) Domain 2
  • Stacks 8 or more wooden blocks without falling
  • Holds child-safe scissors and snips along lines
  • Hops on one foot forward for 3 steps
Social-Emotional & Self-Care Domain 3
  • Shares crayons and takes turns during group play
  • Washes and dries hands with minimal assistance
